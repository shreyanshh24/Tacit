The problem

Project knowledge is scattered.

  • →What the team knows is split across Jira, Drive, transcripts, repos, and Slack — no single place to ask.
  • →Why decisions were made goes unrecorded; teams repeat solved mistakes.
  • →Context walks out the door with every departure; new plans hide unspoken assumptions.
  • →Onboarding becomes archaeology. Getting an answer means pinging five people.
